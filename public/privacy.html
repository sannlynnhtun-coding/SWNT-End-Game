Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

This policy covers Assembly: Endgame, the word, Myanmar slang and proverb game at swnt-end-game.vercel.app.

Information used

You can play without signing in. Nickname-only play creates an anonymous Supabase account. Optional Google sign-in supplies your Google account identifier, email, name and possibly profile photo URL. Email/password accounts use your email and chosen player name. We use account information to identify your player and save game results. Google sign-in uses basic identity scopes; the game does not request Google Drive, Sheets, Gmail or contacts access.

Public leaderboard

Your confirmed player name, player identifier, wins and best streak can appear publicly in mode-specific rankings. Your email and passwords are not displayed in the leaderboard. Review the player name before saving it.

Storage and service providers

Supabase stores authentication accounts, player names and submitted results. Browser storage holds authentication session data, preferences, local progress, word history and results waiting to upload. Vercel hosts the website. These services process requests and may maintain operational logs under their own policies. The game does not use Google profile data for advertising or AI training.

Retention and deletion

Signing out ends the browser session but does not delete online results. Clearing browser data removes local progress and may remove access to a nickname-only account. To request deletion of your account and leaderboard records, contact the developer. Identity verification may be needed before deleting records.
